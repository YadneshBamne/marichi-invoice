Company roles and the agreement stack

Left: who can do what inside a company and how people join. Right: the ten agreements placed on the invoice lifecycle, with who signs each one.

Swipe sideways inside the diagram. The document cards below hold the same facts as text.

Placement on the timeline follows the guide's "when" column. The step references (5, 7, 8, 11) tie a document to the 14-step lifecycle where the guide's step table names the event; the other documents are signed once, outside any single invoice. Each country's lawyer adapts all ten; nothing here is a template to copy blindly.

The ten documents

1. Platform terms and privacy notice

Signup

Signed by Every user

Must do Roles, eligibility, e-signature and e-records consent, data sharing with financiers, consent withdrawal, complaints route.

2. Seller master receivables agreement

Once, at approval

Signed by Seller and funder (or platform as agent)

Must do True-sale or financing characterisation, eligible and excluded invoices, seller warranties, recourse and repurchase events, reserve, pricing, authority to notify and register, audit rights, termination, governing law.

3. Invoice schedule / offer letter

Per invoice

Signed by Seller and funder

Must do Invoice identifiers, face value, advance, reserve, fee, discount rate, day-count basis and days, due date, payee account, recourse status, acceptance timestamp, incorporation of document 2.

4. Notice of assignment and debtor acknowledgement

Per invoice (or per master)

Signed by Sent by seller or funder; buyer acknowledges

Must do Identifies the invoice and the new owner, payment instruction, effective on receipt, buyer states known set-off or claims, no change to currency or place of payment, proof of transfer on request.

5. Buyer acceptance / confirmation

Per invoice

Signed by Buyer (one click, recorded)

Must do Binding if it is an unconditional payment undertaking that waives defences and set-off in a signed writing; fraud and incapacity carved out; consent to assignment; dispute procedure.

6. Buyer programme agreement

Once

Signed by Buyer and funder or agent

Must do Authority to pay, unconditional payment on the due date without set-off, acknowledgement of assignment and registry filing, information exchange, adverse-claims notice.

7. Financier funding agreement and mandate

Once

Signed by Platform and financier

Must do Eligibility criteria, per-buyer limits, funding commitments, servicing and collections, reporting, repurchase pass-through, fees, licence warranty.

8. Collection account / trust agreement

Once

Signed by Platform or funder, account bank, seller

Must do Account title and ring-fencing, who may pay in, sweep timing, waterfall, trust declaration, no set-off by the bank, reconciliation, seller insolvency, signatories.

9. Direct-debit or e-mandate consent

Per mandate

Signed by Payer

Must do Strong authentication at registration, validity period, maximum amount, pre-debit notice, opt-out.

10. Key facts / rate disclosure sheet

Before accepting any offer

Signed by Given to seller; seller acknowledges

Must do One standard format, all-in rate (APR), every charge including contingent ones listed separately, validity of the quote, language the seller understands.
