Reading the diagram: each column is one step, in order. Solid arrows are the main path. Step 11 branches to step 13 when the buyer pays late or disputes. Standard timings published and tracked: buyer acknowledgement within 48 hours (reminders at 24h and 48h, human escalation at 72h); decision within 4 hours of acceptance for auto-approved cases; payout T+1; collections reminders from 3 days before due.

6. Underwriting

Country pack

Who System + analysts

7. Offers and bids

Country packApprover

Who System, financiers, seller

What happens Platform or partner offer plus competing financier bids, shown as net cash today and discount, with a standard disclosure sheet.

Approver Accepting an offer or bid binds the company: any Maker prepares, an Approver (not the same person) commits. A financier bid is also Maker then Approver, inside its mandate and limits.
